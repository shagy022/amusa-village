Welcome to your village

Meet Excalibur, your crimson-clad adventurer. Explore the grounds and step inside any building.

Move — use WASD / arrow keys. Hold Shift to run. Drag to look around.
Zoom — scroll or use + and −.
Enter — walk into a doorway.
Leave — walk to an exit or press Esc.

Church bells follow the real-world Eastern hour. The sound starts after your first game click.

Open meadows at the edges are reserved for your growing town. Visit the general store, walk to the counter and press T to trade. Use People for resident information and the coin button for the treasury.
